## 🎯 Overview & High Concept

In **Sabotage Studio**, players move freely around **The Lot** (a top-down broadcast facility) to operate interactive minigame stations, earn points, and survive sudden studio crises.

Key mechanics include:
* **Free-Roam Exploration**: Navigate room-to-room with dynamic line-of-sight vision cones and fog-of-war.
* **Interactive Workstations**: Solve varied micro-puzzles (wire cutting, frequency tuning, espresso brewing, code cracking) to earn points for your crew score.
* **Live Sabotage Shop**: Spend earned points to launch real-time disruptors against other players (flashbangs, control inversions, ad avalanches, control swaps).
* **Station Swapping**: Dynamic events swap control and station perspectives, forcing real-time communication and adaptiveness.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Host a Room or Join**: One player creates a room and receives a unique 6-character room code. Other players join using the code. No download or registration required!
2. **Lobby Assembly**: Customize your player display name and wait for all crew members (2 to 8 players).
3. **Studio Floor Action**:
   - Use movement keys to walk across the studio lot.
   - Walk up to task stations and hold `E` to charge up and initiate the minigame.
   - Complete tasks accurately and quickly to gain individual and crew points.
4. **Unleash Sabotages**: Open the **Sabotage Shop** (`Tab` key) to purchase visual, input, social, or structural sabotages targeted at rival operators.
5. **Survive Crises**: When a Studio Crisis triggers (e.g. Power Outage or Fire Alarm), coordinate with teammates to resolve the emergency before time runs out.

| Action | Key / Input | Description |

## ⚡ Sabotage Arsenal

Spend points accumulated from completing tasks to attack opponents with live sabotage effects across four distinct categories:

### 👁️ Visual Sabotages
- **Flashbang**: Blinds the target's screen with a intense white flash followed by slow fade recovery.
- **Grease Screen**: Smears realistic oil smudges across the player's viewport.
- **Screen Crack**: Simulates shattered glass web over target workstation controls.
- **Screen Glitch**: Causes chaotic chromatic aberration and digital artifact flickering.
- **Night Vision**: Forces high-contrast neon green grain overlay restricting visual depth.

### 🎮 Input & Movement Sabotages
- **Invert Controls**: Reverses movement directions (`W` moves down, `A` moves right, etc.).
- **Butter Fingers**: Causes interactive items or controls to slip out of cursor grasp.
- **Sticky Drag**: Adds heavy drag resistance and latency to cursor dragging.
- **Ghost Input**: Inject random phantom keypresses into player control streams.

### 🗣️ Social & Psychological Sabotages
- **Control Swap**: Temporarily swaps avatar movement controls between two players!
- **Fake Screen Swap**: Renders a decoy screen of another player's station to disorient target.
- **Ad Avalanche**: Spawns cascade of unskippable retro pop-up advertisements.
- **Soundboard Assault**: Plays loud distractors and comedic studio sound effects.

### 🏗️ Structural & Progress Sabotages
- **Task Rewind**: Resets current station progress bar back to 0%.
- **Station Freeze**: Locks station interactive controls in ice for several seconds.
- **Lag Spike**: Artificially delays network state updates to simulate bad connection latency.

## 🚨 Studio Crises (Co-op Hazards)

When studio tension hits critical limits, automatic **Studio Crises** initiate across the room:

1. **⚡ Power Outage Crisis**: Studio lights blackout! Vision distance drops to minimal radius for 8 seconds.
2. **🔥 Fire Alarm Alert**: High-hazard emergency alarm sounds; all players must reach safety zones or face score penalties.
3. **🎬 Emergency Reshoot (Take-Too-Many)**: High sabotage threshold reached; forces rapid co-op sequence to save production budget.

## 🌐 Multiplayer & P2P Networking

- **Serverless Real-Time P2P**: Powered by **PeerJS** and **WebRTC DataChannels**.
- **Host Authoritative**: The room creator's browser manages player list, score sync, and sabotage dispatching.
- **Lightweight Backend**: Express & SQLite/Supabase handle room registration and code lookups only, ensuring low latency gameplay.

## 💡 Pro Tips & Strategies

1. **Communication is Key**: Call out active sabotages (like Control Swaps or Ad Avalanches) to your teammates immediately.
2. **Save for High-Tier Sabotages**: Inverting controls or triggering a Control Swap right when an opponent is completing a critical task can turn the tide of the match.
3. **Clear Crises Fast**: Resolving Studio Crises quickly prevents team score decay and keeps minigame progress safe.
4. **Watch your Line of Sight**: Pay attention to fog of war—rivals could be sneaking up on your location!
